About Our Company

Precision Electrical Engineering from Foundation to Final Lighting

Who We Are

Founded and led by Ghulam Hussain, GH ELECTRICIAN brings over 8 years of specialized expertise in residential, commercial, and multi-storey building electrical contracting. Supported by a dedicated crew of 3 to 4 skilled technicians, we undertake complete turnkey building wiring contracts—handling every stage from initial wall chasing and concealed underground conduit piping to balanced load distribution boards (MCB/RCCB) and high-end architectural lighting finishes.

Whether you are constructing a new 2–3 storey house, setting up a modern commercial showroom or office, upgrading outdated wiring, or facing an urgent electrical fault, our team delivers safe, code-compliant, and visually stunning results.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, overload-protected concealed electrical networks and luxury final lighting installations with zero compromise on wire gauge quality, safety standards, or project timelines.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted electrical contracting brand known for transforming raw brick structures into smart, brilliantly illuminated modern homes and commercial spaces.

Core Professional Values

Safety First: Proper earthing, load division & circuit protection.
Aesthetic Finish: Clean switchboards & luxury false-ceiling lighting.
Accountability: On-schedule completion & rapid fault response.
